Welcome to the Woodworkers Guild of Arizona

Join fellow makers for an afternoon of live demos, shop tips, and a show-and-tell table. Bring a project you're proud of, or one you're stuck on.

Fall Open Shop Day

When: Saturday, November 14, 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM

Where: Mesa Community Woodshop, 123 Main Street, Mesa, AZ

Before you come

  • Wear: closed-toe shoes and clothes you don't mind getting sawdust on. Tie back long hair, and skip loose sleeves near the machines.
  • Safety glasses: required in the shop. Bring your own, or borrow a pair at the welcome table.
  • Bringing a project? Label it with your name and drop it at the show-and-tell table when you arrive.
  • Arrival: check in at the welcome table when you arrive. The tour starts at 10:00 AM.

Agenda

  1. 10:00 AM Welcome, coffee & shop tour
  2. 10:30 AM Live demo: hand-cut dovetails
  3. 11:30 AM Sharpening station: chisels & plane irons
  4. 12:30 PM Lunch & show-and-tell table
  5. 1:30 PM Open Q&A and project troubleshooting
